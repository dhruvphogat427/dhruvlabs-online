About FaceMap

Analysis that stays on your phone.

FaceMap analyzes a photo you take or choose, using an on-device 468-point face mesh model, to estimate your face shape and a geometry-based Face Score, then recommends hairstyles, beards, and glasses that complement your proportions.

Every analysis runs locally on your device — no photo is ever uploaded anywhere. FaceMap is a product by DhruvLabs, an independent product studio building AI-powered apps and digital experiences.
