Haircut guide

The best haircut for your face shape

The right haircut adds what your face shape lacks: height for a round face, width for a long one, softness for a strong jaw. Pick your shape below for the men's and women's cuts that suit it, the ones to skip, and what to say at the barber.

The one rule: balance your proportions

Every face-shape haircut tip comes from one idea. Look at what your face already has a lot of, then use the haircut to add the opposite. Volume on top makes a face look longer. Volume at the sides makes it look wider. Texture and soft lines calm a strong jaw; sharp lines and height add structure to a soft one.

Your hair type matters as much as your face shape. Thick hair holds height easily, fine hair often looks better shorter with texture, and curly hair adds width on its own. Use the picks below as a starting point, then adjust for your hair.

Haircuts for each face shape

Haircuts for an oval face

Longer than wide, balanced, rounded jaw.

An oval face is the easiest shape to cut for. Almost every style works, so choose by hair type and how much styling time you want.

Men

Textured crop, side part, quiff, slick back, or a short buzz cut. All of them keep the natural balance.

Women

Long layers, a lob, curtain bangs, a sleek bob or a pixie cut. Most lengths and partings work.

Ask your barber

"Texture on top, tapered sides, keep the shape balanced." Any length is fine.

Go easy on: a heavy, blunt fringe that covers the whole forehead and hides the balanced proportions.

Haircuts for a round face

About as wide as long, full cheeks, soft jaw.

Add height and angles so the face looks longer and more defined. Keep the sides tighter than the top.

Men

Quiff, pompadour, faux hawk, or an undercut with volume on top. Short sides, such as a fade or a #1 to #2 clipper guard.

Women

Long layers that fall past the chin, a deep side part, or a lob cut longer at the front. Volume at the crown helps.

Ask your barber

"Height on top, tight sides, and a side part." The asymmetry breaks up the roundness.

Go easy on: chin-length bobs, blunt full fringes and one-length buzz cuts, which all repeat the round outline.

Haircuts for a square face

About as wide as long, strong and angular jaw.

A square face already has a strong jaw and structure. Add softness and a little length rather than more corners.

Men

Textured crop, messy fringe, a soft side part, or medium-length hair with some movement.

Women

Soft waves, long layers, side-swept bangs or a wispy lob that falls below the jaw.

Ask your barber

"Texture instead of sharp lines, and keep the sides a little longer than a skin fade."

Go easy on: flat tops, very sharp geometric lines and a blunt bob that ends right at the jaw.

Haircuts for an oblong (long) face

Clearly longer than wide, even widths.

A long face needs width, not height. Keep volume at the sides and use a fringe to shorten the face.

Men

Textured fringe, French crop, side part with fuller sides, or a medium crop. Keep the top fairly low.

Women

Shoulder-length cuts with waves, curtain bangs, or a bob with volume at the sides.

Ask your barber

"Keep the height down and leave some fullness at the sides. A fringe is fine."

Go easy on: tall pompadours and quiffs, very long straight hair and high skin fades, which all add length.

Haircuts for a heart-shaped face

Wide forehead, narrower jaw, pointed chin.

Balance a wider forehead by adding fullness lower down, around the jaw and chin.

Men

Side-swept fringe, medium length, or a textured crop that keeps some length at the sides.

Women

A chin-length bob, long layers that start at the chin, or side-swept bangs.

Ask your barber

"Less volume at the temples, a bit more fullness around the jaw."

Go easy on: lots of height on top with very short sides, and slicked-back styles that widen the forehead.

Haircuts for a diamond face

Wide cheekbones, narrower forehead and jaw.

Add fullness at the forehead and chin so they balance wide cheekbones.

Men

A fringe, textured top, side part, or longer sides that can be tucked behind the ears.

Women

A chin-length bob, a deep side part, or bangs that add width at the forehead.

Ask your barber

"Width at the forehead, and don't cut the sides too tight at the cheekbones."

Go easy on: slicked-back styles with tight sides and a centre part with flat sides, which make the cheekbones look wider.

How to ask your barber for the right cut

Barbers work fastest with a photo and a few clear numbers. Bring a picture of the cut, say which face shape you have, and use clipper guard numbers for the sides.

  • #1 guard: about 3 mm (1/8 inch). Very short, skin shows through.
  • #2 guard: about 6 mm (1/4 inch). Short and neat.
  • #3 guard: about 10 mm (3/8 inch). Short but full.
  • #4 guard: about 13 mm (1/2 inch). Soft and longer at the sides.
  • Fade vs taper: a fade goes down to skin; a taper gets shorter but keeps some hair at the bottom.

Adjust for your hair type

  • Thick hair: holds height and texture well; ask for thinning on top if it puffs out at the sides.
  • Fine hair: shorter, textured cuts look fuller than long, flat ones.
  • Curly hair: adds width naturally, so long and oblong faces benefit; round faces should keep the sides shorter.
  • Receding hairline: short textured crops and a light fringe work better than slicked-back styles.

Common questions

What haircut suits a round face for men?

Cuts with height on top and short sides, such as a quiff, pompadour or faux hawk with a fade, make a round face look longer. A side part helps too.

Which haircut makes a face look slimmer?

Cuts with volume on top and tighter sides, or long layers that fall past the chin, draw the eye up and down and make the face look slimmer.

What is the best haircut for a long face?

Cuts that add width and keep height low: a fringe or French crop for men, shoulder-length waves or curtain bangs for women.

How do I know which haircut suits me?

Start with your face shape, then adjust for your hair type and how much styling you want. Measuring your face, or letting an app measure it, is more reliable than guessing from a photo.
