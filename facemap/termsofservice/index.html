2 The service

FaceMap analyzes a photo you provide, using an on-device face mesh model, to estimate your face shape, a geometry-based "Face Score," and style recommendations (hairstyles, beards, glasses). All analysis runs locally on your device.

The free tier shows your face shape, Face Score, and full style browsing. Premium unlocks your full face-metrics breakdown and complete analysis report.
