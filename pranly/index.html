Meet Pranly

Breathe better. Build a daily habit.

Breathing Exercises

Pranly is a calm, thoughtfully designed breathing app that helps you create a simple daily breathing practice. Follow a gently animated breathing orb, choose your routine, and take a few mindful minutes for yourself.

Coming soon Get Pranly Coming to Google Play and the App Store
What Pranly does

A few mindful minutes, made simple.

Clear guidance, a gentle animation, and a routine you can stick with.

Guided breathing

Clear “Breathe In,” “Hold,” and “Breathe Out” instructions with synchronized animations.

Four breathing routines

Relaxing Breath, Equal Breathing, Box Breathing, and Deep Yogic Breathing.

Flexible sessions

Choose 1, 3, 5, 10, or 15 minutes to fit the time you have.

Streaks and progress

Build streaks, celebrate milestones, and view completed practice days, session totals, and breathing minutes.

Daily reminders

Set an optional local reminder at a time that suits you.

Comfort and offline

Light and dark themes, optional gentle sound and haptics. Core sessions, history, and preferences work without internet.

About Pranly

No account. No fuss.

No account is required. Your breathing history and preferences stay on your device. There are no subscriptions, and purchase prices are shown in your store's local currency.

Android: Breathing sessions are free. Unobtrusive banner ads may appear outside the breathing flow, and an optional one-time Remove Ads purchase removes them.

iOS: Completely ad-free. Enjoy one full completed breathing session free, then unlock continued practice with a one-time lifetime purchase.

Wellness notice: Pranly provides general wellness breathing exercises. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or cure any condition.
