Mileback

Privacy Policy

How Mileback, developed under the DSLR Labs brand, collects and handles your data.

Last updated: 1 October 2026

1 Overview

Mileback is developed and operated by Lilu Ram, doing business as DSLR Labs (“DSLR Labs”, “we”, “us”, or “our”).

Mileback is an iPhone app that logs the drives you take for work, turns business miles into a tax deduction estimate at the IRS standard mileage rate, keeps track of work expenses and receipts, and creates reports for your taxes.

Mileback has no account, no sign-in and no Mileback server. Everything you record is stored on your iPhone. This policy explains exactly what the app touches, what it does with it, and what it never does. It is written to match the app's actual behavior, not a generic template.

2 What Mileback processes

Location
Used to detect when you start and stop driving and to measure each trip's distance and route. While you're parked, Mileback only watches a small area around where you stopped; precise GPS runs only while a drive is in progress. Trips and routes are saved on your iPhone only.
Motion & Fitness activity
If you allow it, Mileback asks iOS whether the phone was in a vehicle, on a bike or on foot during a finished drive, so bike rides and runs aren't logged as drives. The activity data is read on your iPhone and not stored or sent anywhere.
Trips, classifications and notes
Distance, times, start and end place names, business or personal, the gig platform you pick, and any notes you add. Stored on your iPhone only.
Expenses and receipt photos
Photos you take or pick of receipts, and the merchant, amount, date and category. Receipt text is read on your iPhone using Apple's on-device text recognition. Photos and details are stored on your iPhone only.
Vehicles, odometer readings and places
Vehicle names, odometer readings you enter, and places you save (such as Home or a pickup zone) with their location. Stored on your iPhone only.
Onboarding answers and settings
The platforms you drive for, your weekly-miles estimate, tracking rules, theme and reminder choices. Stored on your iPhone only.

3 What we don't collect

  • No name, email or phone number. There is no account or sign-in in Mileback.
  • No advertising, no advertising identifiers and no ad SDKs. Mileback does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • No third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK, so no usage events are sent off your device.
  • We never sell or share your trips, locations, receipts or any other data. We don't receive them in the first place.
  • No contacts, microphone or health data. When you pick a receipt from your photos, only the photo you choose is shared with the app.

4 Where your data lives

Everything Mileback stores lives in a database and files inside the app on your iPhone. The Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets read a summary (such as this month's deduction) from storage shared only between Mileback and its widgets on the same iPhone.

Backup to your own iCloud Drive. If you are signed in to iCloud with iCloud Drive turned on, Mileback keeps a copy of its data (your trips, expenses, receipt photos, vehicles, places and settings) in a private Mileback folder in your own iCloud Drive, so it isn't lost if you delete the app or move to a new iPhone. It is updated after each saved trip and when you leave the app, and you can restore it from Settings → Data & privacy or when you reinstall. This folder belongs to your iCloud account and is stored by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. DSLR Labs has no access to it and Mileback has no servers of its own.

If you have iCloud Backup turned on, Apple also includes Mileback's data in your iPhone's encrypted device backup, like other apps. DSLR Labs cannot access that either.

5 When data leaves your iPhone

Place names (Apple)
To show a street or neighborhood name for where a trip started and ended, Mileback uses Apple's location services to look up the name for those coordinates. Apple handles these lookups under its own privacy policy; they are not sent to DSLR Labs.
Reports and images you share
PDF and CSV tax reports, monthly recap cards and Year in review images leave your iPhone only when you choose to share or save them through the iOS share sheet, and only to the destination you pick. Recap and Year in review images never include addresses or real routes.
Your iCloud Drive backup
A copy of your Mileback data is saved to your own iCloud Drive, as described in "Where your data lives". It goes only to your iCloud account, never to DSLR Labs. Without iCloud Drive turned on, nothing is backed up this way.
App updates (Expo)
When it opens, Mileback checks Expo's update service (expo.dev) for small app updates. The check sends technical information only, such as the app version and device platform (and, like any internet request, an IP address). It never includes your trips, locations, receipts or any other data you record.
Purchases
See "Purchases and subscription data" below.

6 Permissions we ask for

Location (Always)
To log drives automatically, including when the app is closed. You can use Mileback without it by adding trips by hand.
Motion & Fitness
Optional. Helps tell driving apart from cycling or running, and confirms slow drives in heavy traffic. Motion data is read on your iPhone and never leaves it.
Notifications
Optional. For "Trip logged" alerts, the monthly recap, a year-end odometer reminder, and a reminder before a free trial ends. All are local notifications created on your iPhone.
Camera and Photos
Only when you scan or pick a receipt.

You can change any of these at any time in your iPhone's Settings app.

7 Purchases and subscription data

Mileback Pro is an optional subscription sold through Apple's App Store and managed with RevenueCat. Apple processes the payment: Mileback never sees or stores your card or payment details. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your App Store purchase receipts and basic device and app information so the app can tell whether Pro is active. It never receives your trips, locations, receipts or any other data you record in the app. Apple's and RevenueCat's handling of this information is governed by their own privacy policies.

8 Third parties

Apple
Location services and place-name lookups, on-device text recognition for receipts, Motion & Fitness activity, notifications, iCloud Drive and iCloud Backup (if you use them) and App Store payments.
Expo
Delivers app updates. Receives technical information only (see "App updates" above).
RevenueCat
Manages the Pro subscription status. Receives purchase information and an anonymous identifier only.

9 Your controls

  • Delete a trip or expense: open it and tap Delete.
  • Delete everything: Settings → Data & privacy → Delete all data permanently erases all trips, expenses, receipt photos, vehicles and places from your iPhone.
  • Stop tracking: Settings → Tracking rules → turn off Automatic detection, or change Location access in your iPhone's Settings.
  • Export a copy: Settings → Data & privacy → Export a copy creates a PDF or CSV you can keep.
  • Back up or restore: Settings → Data & privacy → Back up now, or Restore from iCloud.
  • Delete the iCloud Drive backup: iPhone Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage (or Storage) → Mileback → Delete Data. You can also turn off iCloud Drive for Mileback there.
  • Uninstall: deleting the app removes its data from your iPhone. Your iCloud Drive backup stays in your iCloud account until you delete it as above, so you can restore it if you reinstall. Copies in an iCloud device backup are managed by Apple in your iPhone's Settings.

10 Children's privacy

Mileback is made for adults who drive for work and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and because Mileback has no server, there is no server-side data about anyone.

11 Changes to this policy

We will update this page if what Mileback collects or how it's used changes, and note the date of the most recent revision above.

12 Contact
