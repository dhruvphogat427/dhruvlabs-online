2 The service

Mileback logs the drives you take, lets you sort them as business or personal, keeps track of work expenses and receipts, and estimates your mileage deduction at the IRS standard mileage rate. It creates reports you can use with your tax records. Your data is stored on your iPhone.

The free version lets you add trips by hand, sort trips, track expenses and see your totals. Mileback Pro adds automatic trip tracking with the Live Activity, receipt scanning, and PDF and CSV reports.
