About Amoro

Someone who remembers.

Choose his name, look, personality and voice, then talk. He remembers the things you tell him, notices when you have been away, and texts back in your own style, whether that is English, Hinglish or another language.

Amoro is entertainment for adults. He is an AI companion, not a therapist or a real person, and he is flirty but never explicit. The app shows help-line information if you tell him you may hurt yourself.

Amoro is developed and operated by Lilu Ram, doing business as DSLR Labs, an independent developer building AI-powered apps and digital experiences.
